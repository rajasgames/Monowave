# Privacy

Monowave is built from the ground up to respect your privacy.

1. **No Accounts:** You do not need to log in to use the app.
2. **No Analytics:** There are no telemetry, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs included in this app.
3. **Local Storage:** All your listening history, playlists, and settings are stored locally on your device.
4. **Direct Connection:** When you stream or search for music, the app connects directly to YouTube endpoints using NewPipe Extractor. No intermediary servers are involved.
